Dellinoo — Privacy Policy

Draft — last edited 2026-10-05

Draft notice: This is a starting draft written for Facebook App Review and general use. It has not been reviewed by the client or a lawyer. Confirm the contact details and data practices below are accurate before this app is released publicly.

Who we are

Dellinoo ("we", "us") is an online shop that sells clothing, shoes, electronics and other goods, sourced from China and delivered to customers in Zimbabwe. This app is designed and developed by Vizion.

Information we collect

When you create an account or place an order, we collect:

  • Your name, email address and phone number, when you sign up (including via Google Sign-In).
  • Delivery addresses you save, and order details, when you place an order.
  • Your wishlist (the products you save and their price at the time), so it follows you to other devices and so we can tell you when something on it gets cheaper.
  • Details you give us when you request an item (a link or description) or an invoice (the name, address and tax number to bill).
  • Product reviews you write, which are shown publicly with your name.
  • A notification token for your device, if you are signed in, so we can send you notifications (see below), and a history of the notifications we have sent you.
  • Crash reports and basic usage statistics (such as which screens are opened and when items are added to the cart or bought), through Google's Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics, to find bugs and improve the app.
  • Recently viewed products, recent searches and your app settings, stored only on your device.

We do not collect payment card details directly — payments are handled by our payment provider (Paynow), which has its own privacy practices.

How we use your information

We use your information to create your account, process and deliver your orders, provide customer support, and let you sign in with Google or Facebook if you choose to. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Notifications

With your permission, the app sends notifications to your phone:

  • Order updates — payment confirmations, delivery progress, reminders about an unpaid order, quotes for items you asked us to find, and invoices you requested.
  • Price drops — when something on your wishlist gets cheaper.
  • Deals & offers — occasional sales and new arrivals.

You can turn price drops and deals off in the app under Profile → Settings → Notifications, and turn any of them off in your phone's own notification settings. Notifications are delivered through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. A copy of each personal notification is kept in the app's notification list so you can see it later; you can delete them there.

Services we use

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (accounts, orders, wishlist and notifications). Payments are processed by Paynow. Our payment and notification service runs on Cloudflare. These providers process data on our behalf to run the app.

Sign in with Google or Facebook

If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from that provider to create or match your Dellinoo account. We do not post to your Google or Facebook account on your behalf.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account and order information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to fulfil orders and meet legal requirements. Deleting your account removes your saved addresses, wishlist, notification history and device notification tokens; order and invoice records are kept for accounting. You can delete your account in the app or request deletion at any time — see our Data Deletion Instructions.
