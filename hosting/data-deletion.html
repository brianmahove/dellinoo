Dellinoo — Data Deletion Instructions

Draft — last edited 2026-09-29

Draft notice: Written to satisfy Facebook/Google App Review requirements — not reviewed by a lawyer.

Delete your account yourself, in the app

Open Dellinoo, go to Profile → your account, and tap Delete account at the bottom. This immediately removes your account and your saved delivery addresses. You'll need to sign in again first if it's been a while since you last did.

Or, request it by email or WhatsApp

If you can't access the app, send a deletion request using either method below instead:

Email
Send a request to mahovebrian@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Dellinoo account".
WhatsApp
Message us on WhatsApp with your account's registered phone number and "Delete my account".

We will confirm your identity, then delete your account and personal data within 30 days.

What's kept

Either way, completed order records are kept afterwards for accounting and legal purposes — your name, address and items on past orders aren't erased, but they're no longer linked to an active account you can sign in to.

If you signed in with Google or Facebook

Deleting your Dellinoo account does not affect your Google or Facebook account. To remove Dellinoo's access to your Google or Facebook account entirely, remove it from that provider's own "Apps & websites" or "Connected apps" settings.
